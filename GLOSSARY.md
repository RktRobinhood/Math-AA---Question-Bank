# Question Bank glossary

Use these names when discussing the real exam archive, generated Practice, and Calculator Skills. Rules and workflows live in the [README](README.md) and [decision records](docs/adr/README.md).

## Exam archive

**Course**: One of Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches SL (AA SL), Analysis and Approaches HL (AA HL), Applications and Interpretation SL (AI SL), or Applications and Interpretation HL (AI HL), in the first-assessment 2021 syllabus. A **Paper** and a **Syllabus code** are interpreted within a course.
_Avoid_: “AA” or “AI” alone when the level matters.

**Paper**: An official numbered component of an IB examination. Paper number is not a synonym for calculator policy.

**Exam question**: A real past-paper question with its official wording, marks and markscheme. It remains an exam question when assigned in **Practice**.
_Avoid_: “Practice question” on its own when the item's origin matters.

**Exam appearance**: One sitting, **Course**, **Paper**, and printed question number at which an **Exam question** appeared. A shared SL/HL question can have two appearances without being two stored questions.

**Question part**: A separately labelled, marked part of an **Exam question**. The same question can assess different skills in different parts.

**Syllabus statement**: A curriculum statement identified consistently across the **Courses** that share it; for example, infinite geometric series is statement `SL1.8`. Its **Syllabus code** can vary by course.

**Syllabus code**: The statement's number in a particular **Course**. For example, AA `1.8` and AI HL `1.11` refer to the same **Syllabus statement**.
_Avoid_: “statement ID” for a course's printed number.

**Whole-topic filter**: One Boolean selection on the Questions page. A question matches a topic if any of its tagged parts carries a code in that topic. AND requires the topic, OR puts it in the same “Any of” group as other OR selections, and NOT also excludes questions where it appears as a secondary tag. Subtopics remain independent.

## Practice and Calculator Skills

**Practice**: The learner's study area for **Generated exercises** and, where allowed, **Exam question** fallback and review. The two origins stay distinct.

**Generated exercise**: An original exercise produced for a **Course**, **Practice level**, and numerical case. It is not an **Exam question**, even when designed to assess the same skill.

**Question type**: A specific skill a student can be asked to demonstrate within a **Syllabus statement**, such as solving a quadratic equation. It can have several **Question forms**.

**Question form**: A distinct way to ask a **Question type**, for example by changing the unknown or what is given as a graph, equation or context. New numbers alone are another instance of the same form.
_Avoid_: “Variant” without saying whether the form changed or only the numbers did.

**Practice level**: A difficulty rung in **Practice**, not the SL/HL of a **Course** or a **Paper** number. A fixed band offers a set of rungs without advancing mastery.

**Practice scope**: The **Course** and **Syllabus codes** selected for study, with optional restrictions on the **Question types** to include.

**Calculator Skills**: The standalone library of lessons on specific TI-Nspire CX II-T workflows. Lessons have **Watch**, and **Try** where the relevant calculator workflow can be practised.
_Avoid_: “Scratchpad” or “full emulator” for the lessons.

**Watch**: The worked problem in a **Calculator Skills** lesson. It states what to find, shows the handheld method and interprets the result.

**Matched practice case**: A distinct original **Try** problem that exercises the same mathematical decision, calculator workflow and interpretation as **Watch**, at the relevant **Course** level.

**Watch player**: The shared **Watch** surface and its controls. A lesson supplies a worked example, chapters, and key presses, not a new player. The same player can also open from eligible **Practice**.

**Calculator framing**: **Full calculator** shows the handset and its keys. **Screen only** crops to its LCD. Both are distinct from **Fullscreen**, which expands the entire player.

**Captions (CC)**: On-screen text of the spoken guidance. Captions can be shown or hidden without resizing the calculator; they are not the key-press trail or notes.

**Playback pacing**: The control for the time between spoken steps. It changes the **Teaching hold**, not the speed or pitch of a recording.

**Teaching hold**: The part after a spoken step where the learner can see the calculator actions, read the result or follow along.

**Try**: The independent exercise in a **Calculator Skills** lesson.

**Scratchpad**: The blank calculator a student operates themselves. It is neither worked help nor the **Try** exercise from a **Calculator Skills** lesson.

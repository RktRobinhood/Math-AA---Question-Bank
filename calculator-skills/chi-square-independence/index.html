    What makes the test valid?
    Observed counts
    Each independent observation belongs to exactly one row and one column. Enter counts only, excluding row totals, column totals and percentages. The categories must be mutually exclusive within each variable.
    Expected counts
    Under independence, an expected count is its row total times its column total, divided by the grand total. In this IB-style lesson, every expected count must be greater than 5. Use a defensible study design; counts alone cannot establish independent observations. Never combine unlike categories just to obtain a test result.
    Meaning of PVal
    Assuming independence and the test conditions, it is the probability of a chi-square statistic at least as large as the one observed. A large p-value does not prove independence, and association does not establish causation.

Find an intersection

Bracket one crossing of two graphs on a TI-Nspire CX II-T.

Worked example

Find the crossing on the right

f₁(x) = x²
f₂(x) = 2x + 3

    Keyboard: M, 6, 4; arrow keys move the cursor, or type a bound. Enter confirms. Use the onscreen Tab key to open f₂.

    Round to three significant figures if needed. You can also enter the displayed values to check your work.

    Hint

    What do the coordinates mean?

    The x-coordinate solves f₁(x) = f₂(x). The y-coordinate is their common output. Report both if asked for the point.

Find an intersection

Find each crossing of two graphs separately on a TI-Nspire CX II-T.

Worked example

Find the right crossing, then the left

f₁(x) = x²
f₂(x) = 2x + 3

Keyboard: M, 6, 4; arrow keys move the cursor, or type a bound. Enter confirms. Use the onscreen Tab key to open f₂.

What to look for

Where: Graphs → Menu → 6: Analyze Graph → 4: Intersection. Why: Put one bound on each side of the crossing you want. Repeat with a separate bracket for any other crossing. What you see: The x-coordinate solves f₁(x) = f₂(x). The y-coordinate is their common output.

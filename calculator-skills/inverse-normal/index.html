      Tail practice

      Sensor lifetimes are normally distributed with mean 40 months and standard deviation 5 months. Find the cutoff exceeded by 20% of sensors.

      Hint

      If the named area is to the right, subtract it from one before entering the area in Inverse Normal.

      Use a different example

      Enter the left area, mean and standard deviation directly in the handheld. The graph below shows the area you entered. The answer checker above is only for the practice prompt.

      Keep in your notes

      Inverse Normal returns a value, in the units of the data. Enter invNorm(left area, mean, SD). Confirm the dialog, then press Enter again to calculate.

      • Below the cutoff: enter that probability directly.
      • Above the cutoff: enter 1 minus the stated right-tail probability.
      • A left area greater than 0.5 gives a cutoff above the mean.

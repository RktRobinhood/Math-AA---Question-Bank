    Why these settings?
    PV and FV
    From your perspective, the opening deposit leaves you now (negative PV); the balance is money you can receive later (positive FV).
    N, PpY and CpY
    N counts payment periods, not years. PpY is payment periods per year; CpY counts compoundings per year. Here we set them equal so N counts compounding periods, even though Pmt is zero. The nominal rate I(%) is entered per year as a percentage.
    Pmt and PmtAt
    Set Pmt to zero for no recurring payments. With no payments, the BEGIN/END timing does not change the result.

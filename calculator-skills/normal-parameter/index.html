    Your turn

    Find the mean

    A normal distribution has standard deviation 4, and 75% of its values are above 37. Find its mean.

    Hint

    Convert to a left-tail area if needed. Find z with mean 0 and standard deviation 1, then rearrange x = μ + σz.

    Set your example

    Set different known values. Work out the missing parameter on the handheld.

    Keep in your notes

    Find the standard score first with invNorm(left area, 0, 1). Then use x = μ + σz.

    • Mean: μ = x − σz.
    • Standard deviation: σ = (x − μ)/z. The spread must be positive. At the median, if the cutoff equals the mean then any positive spread works; if they differ, the values are inconsistent.
    • Use the stored Ans, not a rounded copy of the display.

    Captions only
    Why a difference of squares?

    For a washer, subtract the area of the hole from the area of the outside disk. Squaring the gap between the radii would give a different shape. A disk has inner radius zero. Around the y-axis the corresponding radii are horizontal distances, expressed in y.

    Note down: V = π∫(R² − r²) dx for this x-axis setup.

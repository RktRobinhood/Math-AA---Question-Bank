    Why squared distance?

    Taking a square root does not change the order of non-negative values. Smallest squared distance means nearest site. A tie between two sites marks their shared boundary only if neither is farther than a third site. When tied sites disagree, give no unique estimate unless the question gives a tie-break rule. Tied sites with the same reading still give the same estimate.

Calculator notes

On the handheld, use the touchpad pointer to click RAD or DEG on the Calculator page. In Try, click or tap the same spot. To choose Gradian or change defaults, use Home → 5: Settings → 2: Document Settings → Angle. Choose the unit requested in the question. Inverse trig takes a ratio and returns one principal angle. The superscript −1 means inverse, not reciprocal. If the question asks for all solutions, check its full interval.

Caption transcript

Find the principal angle for sine theta equals zero point six, in degrees. Check the top-right rad or deg label. Use the touchpad to move the pointer to rad. Click the touchpad centre. Rad changes to deg. Press trig. Move down to inverse sine and press Enter. The minus one means inverse, not reciprocal. Type zero point six and press Enter. Inverse sine takes a ratio and gives an angle. About thirty-six point eight seven degrees. That is one principal angle. If asked for all solutions, check the whole interval.

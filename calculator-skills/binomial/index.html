    Keep in your notes

    CDF adds the probabilities of all integer counts in a range, including both bounds. The possible counts are 0 through n.

    • At least k: k to n. At most k: 0 to k.
    • More than k: start at k + 1. Fewer than k: end at k − 1.
    • Complement: subtract the probability of the counts outside your event from 1.

| Beat | Spoken script | What the learner must see | Teaching hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | We’re evaluating this fraction on a TI-Nspire, then rounding the result to three significant figures. Press Home. Choose one for New, then press Enter on Add Calculator. | Separate Home, 1 and Enter highlights; show each menu. | 4 s |
| 2 | To get a proper fraction, press the red Control key and then Divide. The cursor starts above the fraction bar. Put both factors there, and keep the denominator below the bar. | Show Ctrl and Divide, then the numerator cursor before its location is spoken. | 4 s |
| 3 | Find the E E key at the far left of the letter keys. It means “times ten to the power of”. Type four, E E, five. On the screen, four E five means four times ten to the fifth. | Show 4, EE and 5 separately in the key trail and the resulting characters on the LCD. | 5 s |
| 4 | Still on top, press Multiply. Then type three, E E, the negative key next to Enter, and two. The negative key belongs to the exponent. It doesn’t subtract a term. | Show Multiply, 3, EE, negative, and 2 separately. Show 3E−2 on the LCD. | 5 s |
| 5 | Press Down to move below the fraction bar. Now type two, E E, three. Check that both factors are above the bar before pressing Enter. | Show Down, 2, EE and 3; hold the completed fraction. Press Enter after the check. | 5 s |
| 6 | The calculator displays six. For three significant figures, start at the six and write six point zero zero. The two zeros after the decimal point show the requested precision. | LCD readout 6, then show written answer 6.00 and identify its three digits. | 5 s |

Check that every key locator matches the real keypress. Give students time after the result and before the next instruction. Keep the learner’s chosen frame steady throughout.

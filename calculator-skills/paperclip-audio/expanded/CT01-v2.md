| Beat | Spoken script | What the learner must see | Teaching hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | We’re evaluating this fraction on a TI-Nspire, then rounding the result to three significant figures. Press Home. Choose one for New, then press Enter on Add Calculator. | Full handheld: separate Home, 1 and Enter highlights; show each menu. | 4 s |
| 2 | To get a proper fraction, press the red Control key and then Divide. The cursor starts above the fraction bar. Put both factors there, and keep the denominator below the bar. | Full handheld through Ctrl and Divide, then the LCD with the numerator cursor. | 4 s |
| 3 | Find the E E key at the far left of the letter keys. It means “times ten to the power of”. Type four, E E, five. On the screen, four E five means four times ten to the fifth. | Full frame to locate EE, then LCD for the actual characters. Press 4, EE, 5 separately. | 5 s |
| 4 | Still on top, press Multiply. Then type three, E E, the negative key next to Enter, and two. The negative key belongs to the exponent. It doesn’t subtract a term. | Show Multiply, 3, EE, negative, and 2 separately. Full frame for the negative key; LCD for 3E−2. | 5 s |
| 5 | Press Down to move below the fraction bar. Now type two, E E, three. Check that both factors are above the bar before pressing Enter. | Show Down, 2, EE and 3; hold the completed fraction. Press Enter after the check. | 5 s |
| 6 | The calculator displays six. For three significant figures, start at the six and write six point zero zero. The two zeros after the decimal point show the requested precision. | LCD readout 6, then show written answer 6.00 and identify its three digits. | 5 s |

Check that every key locator matches the real keypress. Give students time after the result and before the next instruction. Keep the learner’s manual camera override. The pause preference never changes speech; CC contains actual recorded speech only. Do not populate Try with the answer.

# CT06 v2: Solve an equation numerically

Separate Paperclip recording draft. Preserve the signed CT06 MP3, captions
and measured cue map until a new take is approved and tested. Targets: about
1:40–2:00 including actual key animation and inspection; narration at native 1×.
Do not force a view change. Keep the complete key trail while the learner
chooses Screen or Full. Directions in the Replay column are not spoken.

| Beat | Spoken script | Replay action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We’ll use Numerical Solve to find one solution to an equation. The same equation can have another solution, so we’ll run the command on two different intervals. Start in Calculator. Press Menu just below and to the right of the touchpad, then choose three for Algebra and one for Numerical Solve. | Show Calculator; Menu, 3, 1 independently. Wait at the empty command before typing. |
| 2 | Type two raised to the power x. Use the power key to make the exponent. After x, press Right to bring the cursor back down. Then enter equals x plus four. Add a comma and x to tell the calculator which variable to solve for. | Show 2, power, x, Right, =, x, +, 4, comma, x individually. Make the caret movement visible before equals. |
| 3 | We’ll look between two and three first. Put the lower bound before the upper bound. Type comma, two, comma, three. Then close the bracket and press Enter. | Show comma, 2, comma, 3 and ); inspect complete command, then Enter. |
| 4 | The calculator gives one solution between two and three. That doesn’t mean it’s the only solution to the equation. Keep the displayed digits while you work. | Hold on the first result and keep its original precision. |
| 5 | To reuse the command, press Up twice to select the input, then Enter to copy it. Move Left inside the bracket. Delete the old bounds and enter negative four, comma, negative three. Use the negative key next to Enter, not subtraction. Press Right, then Enter to solve again. | Show both history selections, copied expression, Left, three Del presses, the two negative entries separated by comma, Right and Enter. Leave first result visible. |
| 6 | Now we have two results from two different intervals. If the question asks for three significant figures, write minus three point nine three and two point seven six. | Show both LCD values and the separate rounded written answers. |

Record cue boundaries and natural pauses, each physical key event and the two
readout cues. Verify both Right-arrow actions and both history selections before
approval. No answer or Watch equation should replace input on an unrelated Try.

On the TI-Nspire CX II-T, press Home, then five for Settings, then two for Document Settings.
Keep Display Digits at Float six. You can see enough digits to round the final answer, without changing the stored value.
Tab to Angle and open the list with the right arrow. Degree is a useful starting setting for A I standard level geometry, bearings and problems marked in degrees.
In A A, start in Radian. The A I higher level exam also assumes radians unless the question indicates otherwise. Switch units whenever the question tells you to.
Tab to Calculation Mode. Choose Approximate if you want decimal results for routine work. Exact Arithmetic is separate and is not required. We leave it off for this setup.
Check R A D in the status bar after returning to Calculator. Seven divided by three now gives a decimal. If the question asks for an exact answer, give seven thirds instead.
You can still enter a stacked fraction. Press Control and Divide. This changes how you enter the expression, not the calculation mode.

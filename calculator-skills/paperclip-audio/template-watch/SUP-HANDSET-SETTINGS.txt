On the TI-Nspire CX II-T, press Home, then five for Settings, then two for Document Settings.
Display Digits changes what you see, not the stored value. Float six shows up to six significant digits. Fix two shows two decimal places.
Tab to Angle. Choose the unit the question uses. For thirty degrees, choose Degree. For an angle written with pi, check whether radians are intended.
Calculation Mode controls how results are returned. Auto can keep a fraction; Approximate returns a decimal. Exact Arithmetic is a separate setting on the non-CAS CX II-T.
Back in Calculator, the status bar should now say DEG for this example. Seven divided by three with Enter gives a fraction. Control Enter requests a decimal for just that result.
A stacked fraction template is an input tool, not a document mode. Press Control and Divide to see the two boxes. MathPrint is a TI-84 setting; there is no MathPrint switch here.

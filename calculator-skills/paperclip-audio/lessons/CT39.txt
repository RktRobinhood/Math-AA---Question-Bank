Shade the area you want. Here, it’s between eighteen and twenty-three.
On a Calculator page, press Menu. Choose Probability, then Distributions, then Normal C D F.
The lower bound is eighteen. The upper bound is twenty-three.
Enter the mean, twenty. Then the standard deviation, two. Use two, not the variance of four.
Confirm the dialog. Press Enter again to calculate. The probability is about zero point seven seven five.
Now shade the upper tail, beyond twenty-two. Open Menu, then Probability, Distributions, and Normal C D F again.
Enter twenty-two as the lower bound. Leave the upper bound at infinity. Keep the mean at twenty and the standard deviation at two.
Confirm, then press Enter again. The upper-tail probability is about zero point one five nine.

Rotate the region from y equals one to y equals two x, with x from one to two inclusive, about the x axis. Each slice has a hole.
The outer radius is two x and the inner radius is one. Enter pi outside the integral. Use a difference of squares, not the square of the gap.
On Calculator, choose Menu then four: Calculus then two: Numerical Definite Integral.
Tab moves from the lower bound one to the upper bound two, then into the integrand.
Enter the square of two times x, minus one squared. The pi outside multiplies every slice. Tab to the differential, type x, then Enter.
The solid has volume about twenty-six point one eight zero cubic units. For a disk touching the axis, the inner radius is zero.

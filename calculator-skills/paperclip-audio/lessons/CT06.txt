On a Calculator page, open Menu, choose Algebra, then Numerical Solve.
Enter the equation, then a comma and the variable you’re solving for.
To limit the search to an interval, add a comma, then enter the lower and upper bounds separated by another comma.
Close the bracket and press Enter. The calculator gives one numerical solution.
If the equation may have more than one solution, use separate intervals and check each result.
Keep the calculator’s full precision while you work, then round the final answer as the question asks.

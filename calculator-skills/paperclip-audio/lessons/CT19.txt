Find the dot product and angle for vector A, with components two, negative one, two, and vector B, with components one, two, negative two. The example is in degrees.
Type A, then Control and Templates to get the assignment symbol. Open Menu, then seven, one, one. Use one row and three columns.
Store B as another matrix with one row and three columns. Use the negative key for negative entries.
Choose Menu, then seven for Matrix and Vector, the letter C for Vector, then three for Dot Product. Enter A followed by a comma and B.
Use inverse cosine, not the reciprocal of cosine. Control and the divide key open a fraction. Put the dot product on top.
Below, use the norm of A times the norm of B. Open Menu, then seven, then seven for Norms, then one for Norm, to insert each norm.
The dot product is negative four. Both lengths are three, so cosine theta is negative four ninths. The negative cosine gives an obtuse angle.
The angle is about one hundred and sixteen point three eight eight degrees. This is one hundred and sixteen degrees to three significant figures. A zero-length vector has no direction.

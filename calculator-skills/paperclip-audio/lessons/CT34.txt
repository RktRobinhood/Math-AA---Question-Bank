Each response is positive, so we can take its natural logarithm.
Move to column C's name cell. Call the new list L N Y.
In the formula cell, press Control and the e to the x key for natural log. Enter the list named y val and close the bracket.
Enter calculates every log in the new column. Keep the same pairing by row.
On Lists and Spreadsheet, choose Menu, Statistics, Stat Calculations, then Linear Regression in the m x plus b form.
Keep x val as the X list. In the Y menu choose L N Y. Press Enter to fit the transformed response.
The slope is the natural log of two and the intercept is the natural log of three. The saved line predicts the natural log of y.
Exponentiate the slope and intercept to get the multiplier and initial value. Use the original y-scale for predictions.
A log-log power fit would transform x too. Do not use that workflow for this exponential example.

On a Calculator page, open Menu then three: Algebra then two: Solve System of Linear Equations.
Use two equations and keep the variables in x, y order. Confirm to insert the template.
Enter the first equation, including its equals sign. Then press Tab for the second line.
Enter the second equation. It can be in a different linear form. Press Enter to solve.
The order, x then y, tells you what each list entry means.
Substitute the pair into both equations to check.

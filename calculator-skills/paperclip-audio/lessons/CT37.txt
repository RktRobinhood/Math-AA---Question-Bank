We want exactly three seeds to sprout. That means one count, not a range.
On a Calculator page, press Menu. Choose five for Probability, five for Distributions, then the letter D for Binomial P D F.
Enter eight for the number of trials. Tab to the success probability, zero point three. Tab to X Value and enter three.
Press Enter to insert the command. Press Enter again to calculate. The probability of exactly three is about zero point two five four.
At most three would include zero, one, two and three. That’s a cumulative probability. This calculation is for exactly three only.

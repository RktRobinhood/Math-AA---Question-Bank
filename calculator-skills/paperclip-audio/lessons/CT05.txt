Deposit one thousand for two years at six percent nominal interest, compounded monthly. There are no later payments.
On a Calculator page, press Menu then eight: Finance then one: Finance Solver.
Two years times twelve months gives twenty-four payment periods. Enter six for the annual percentage rate.
Present value is negative one thousand: the deposit leaves you now. Payment is zero because there are no recurring payments.
Skip future value. To count periods in months, set payments per year and compounding periods per year to twelve. Leave payment timing at End; it has no effect without recurring payments.
Move back to FV, then press Enter to solve for the highlighted field.
A positive future value is money you receive at the end. Rounded to two decimal places, the balance is one thousand, one hundred and twenty-seven point one six.

Plot f of x equals x minus three on a Graphs page.
Open Menu then six: Analyze Graph then six: Integral.
This page contains one curve. If prompted on your handheld, select the curve first.
Type zero for the lower bound and press Enter.
Type four for the upper bound and press Enter.
The integral is minus four. The contribution below the x-axis is negative.
For geometric area, split where the graph crosses the axis, at x equals three.
Add the magnitudes: four point five below and zero point five above. Total geometric area is five.

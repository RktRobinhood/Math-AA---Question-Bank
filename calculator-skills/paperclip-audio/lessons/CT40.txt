This time we know the probability, not the cutoff. The seventy-fifth percentile has zero point seven five of the area to its left.
On a Calculator page, press Menu. Choose Probability, then Distributions, then Inverse Normal.
In Area, enter zero point seven five. Enter fifty for the mean and eight for the standard deviation.
Confirm. Press Enter again to calculate. The cutoff is about fifty-five point four, above the mean of fifty.
For the top ten percent, zero point one is on the right. Subtract from one: zero point nine is on the left.
Open Menu, Probability, Distributions, and Inverse Normal again. Enter zero point nine for Area. Keep fifty for the mean and eight for the standard deviation.
Confirm, then press Enter again. The cutoff is about sixty point three, in the original measurement units.

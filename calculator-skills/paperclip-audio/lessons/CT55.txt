In Graphs, enter f one of x equals x plus one and press Enter.
Press Tab. Enter f two of x equals three and press Enter.
From x equals zero to x equals five, the curves cross at x equals two. The upper curve changes there.
Open Menu and choose Analyze Graph, then Bounded Area.
With exactly two eligible curves, the tool selects them automatically.
For the left region, type zero and press Enter.
Type two and press Enter. That stops the region at the crossing.
The left region has area two. Here the horizontal line is on top.
Run Bounded Area again for the right region.
Type two and press Enter. Then type five and press Enter.
The right region has area four point five. Now the sloping line is on top.
Add the positive areas: two plus four point five equals six point five square units.
When the upper curve changes, split there before calculating the area.

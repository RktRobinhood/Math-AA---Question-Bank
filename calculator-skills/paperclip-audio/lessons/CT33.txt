Each row records dough volume at one hour. Every volume must be positive because exponential regression fits the natural log of y.
Open Statistics, then Stat Calculations. Press the letter A, even if the option is below the visible list.
Use the right arrow and Tab. Set the list called x val as the X List and y val as the Y List.
Use each pair once. Save to f one and keep the results in C and D. Press Enter.
Read a and b. The value a is the estimate at x equals zero. It need not equal the measurement there.
A one-unit rise in x multiplies the estimate by b. Here that is about a fifty point seven percent increase.
The r and r squared rows assess the line fitted to x and the natural log of y, not the raw readings.
At x equals two point five the input is inside the observed span. Substituting gives an interpolation.
At x equals six the input is outside the observed span. The model can predict, but the trend may not continue.

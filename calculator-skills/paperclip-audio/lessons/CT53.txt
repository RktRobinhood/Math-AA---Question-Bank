Enter three minus two x in Graphs. The calculator's x is time t, measured in seconds.
For displacement, take one signed integral. Choose Menu then six: Analyze Graph then six: Integral.
Type zero and press Enter for the lower bound. Type four and press Enter for the upper bound.
The signed result is negative four metres. The cart finishes four metres behind its starting point.
The distance needs the reversal time. Choose Menu then six: Analyze Graph then one: Zero.
Move the cursor to either side of the crossing, confirming each bound with Enter.
Press Enter to mark the zero. Velocity is positive before one point five seconds and negative after it.
Repeat Integral from zero to one point five. This positive part is the forward travel.
The forward piece is positive two point two five metres.
Repeat Integral from one point five to four. The second result is negative because motion was backward.
The second signed piece is negative six point two five metres, so the cart travelled six point two five metres backward.
Add magnitudes for distance. Taking the absolute value of the net result would lose the forward travel.
If the graph only touches zero and stays on the same side, the cart stops but does not reverse. Try a fresh model.

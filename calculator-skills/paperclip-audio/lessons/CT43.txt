We want an average, not one observation. Independent population readings have mean eighty and standard deviation twelve.
The sample mean keeps the same centre. Its spread is the standard error: twelve divided by the square root of thirty-six.
For this large sample, the CLT gives an approximately normal mean. A normal population would give an exact normal mean.

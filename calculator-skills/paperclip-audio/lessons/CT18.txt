Point P is at three, two. Site A is at one, one; B is at seven, one; and C is at five, six. Their readings are fifteen, thirty-two and forty-eight. Which site's reading estimates P?
On the Calculator page, enter the square of three minus one, plus the square of two minus one. Close each bracket and press the x squared key, then press Enter. No square root is needed.
Use the same P, but subtract B's coordinates. Square three minus seven, then add the square of two minus one. A negative difference becomes positive when squared.
Now use C. Square three minus five, then add the square of two minus six. Compare all the results, not just A and B.
The squared distances are A: five, B: seventeen, C: twenty. Site A is nearest. The estimate at P is A's reading, fifteen, not an average.
Now move P to four, two. Its offsets from A are three and one; from B they are negative three and one. Enter three squared plus one squared to check both.
For C, the offsets are negative one and negative four. Enter negative one squared plus negative four squared, using the negative key inside each pair of brackets.
A and B tie at ten; C is farther at seventeen. This point lies on their cell boundary. With readings fifteen and thirty-two, no unique estimate exists unless a tie-break rule is given.

Given that the sine of theta is zero point six, find the principal angle in degrees. Check the angle mode first.
Press Home, then five: Settings and two: Document Settings.
Tab to Angle, press Right, select Degree, then tab to OK and confirm.
Return to the current document. Press Trig and choose inverse sine, not sine.
Enter zero point six and press Enter. Inverse sine turns a ratio into an angle, not its reciprocal.
The display is in degrees. The calculator gives one principal angle; check the range for other solutions.

A locker is either ready (A) or in use (B). Columns of T are the current states; rows are the next states. Store T as t.
Type T, then Control and Template for the assignment symbol. On a Calculator page open Menu, then seven for Matrix and Vector, one for Create, and one for Matrix.
Enter zero point eight, zero point three then zero point two, zero point seven. The second column says a locker currently in use moves to Ready with probability zero point three.
Store s using the same menu, with two rows and one column. The locker starts Ready, so enter one above zero.
Enter t times s. The state column belongs on the right. The upper result, zero point eight, is the probability of Ready after one transition.
Enter T to the fourth power. Press Right to leave the exponent, then times S and Enter. Read zero point six two five after four transitions. Do not take the fourth power of each entry.
Repeat with exponent twenty-four. It is very close to zero point six for Ready. For these positive transitions the model tends to a unique steady state.
At equilibrium, flow from Ready to In Use equals flow back. Zero point two times p equals zero point three times the quantity one minus p. So p is three fifths. That is exact; a finite power is only an approximation.

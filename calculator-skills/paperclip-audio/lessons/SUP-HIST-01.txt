On Data and Statistics, press Tab. Select the prepared list reading on the horizontal axis.
Menu, Plot Type, Histogram. The vertical scale counts observations.
Open Plot Properties, Histogram Properties, Bin Settings, Equal Bin Width. Width is the interval size; alignment sets a boundary.
Type ten for width. Tab to Alignment and type ten again. Confirm the settings.
Use Analyze, Graph Trace. Press Right until you reach the class from twenty up to, but not including, thirty.
Five observations fall here. Twenty belongs here; thirty starts the next bin.
Add the bins that end at or below forty. The reading at forty is not included.

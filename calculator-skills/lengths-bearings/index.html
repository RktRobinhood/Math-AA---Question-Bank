    Quadrants, extending the method and sources

    Take α = tan⁻¹(|east| ÷ |north|) in degrees. From north clockwise, the bearing is α in NE, 180° − α in SE, 180° + α in SW, and 360° − α in NW. Use 000, 090, 180 or 270 on an axis. There is no bearing for a purely vertical line. If a rounded bearing reaches 360°, write 000°.

    For two 2D legs from a shared origin, reduce the difference of bearings to the included angle between 0° and 180°. If their lengths are p and q with included angle C, the endpoint separation is √(p² + q² − 2pq cos C). That is a different setup from the perpendicular displacement components in this example.

    What the endpoint means

    In a Graphs entry line, open Math Templates, select the n-piece template, then set the piece count. Enter each expression next to its condition. The union of the two conditions gives the domain. Do not include the same x in both branches.

    The study circles and point checker are lesson overlays, not claims about native TI rendering. The simulator supports two polynomial expressions (up to cubic), decimal endpoints in [−20, 20] and single-sided conditions. This is a lesson, not full calculator firmware.

    Captions only
    Why this order?

    A point written as a 2×1 column goes on the right of the 2×2 transformation matrix. A row of the matrix acts on the column to produce one coordinate. Reversing these two operands cannot work: their two inner dimensions would be 1 and 2.

    Note down: matrix × point column → image column; read top to bottom.

      Mean 20, standard deviation 2. Find P(X > 23).

      Hint

      Shade the side of 23 where the values are greater. The unbounded end needs infinity. Use 2 for the standard deviation, not 4.

      Use a different example

      Enter your bounds, mean and standard deviation directly in the handheld’s Normal Cdf dialog. The answer checker above is for the practice prompt only.

      Keep in your notes

      Normal Cdf gives an area, not the height of the curve. Enter normCdf(lower, upper, mean, SD). Enter the standard deviation, not the variance.

      • Below a bound: use −∞ for the lower endpoint.
      • Above a bound: use +∞ for the upper endpoint, or subtract the probability below the bound from 1.
      • The normal curve is continuous: a single endpoint has probability zero, so including it does not change the result.

Find the fences and whiskers

Point to a mark to inspect it, or use the handheld arrows.

Repeat a value if it occurs more than once.

Hint

Calculate Q₁ − 1.5 × IQR and Q₃ + 1.5 × IQR. Modified whiskers end at the smallest and largest observations that are not outside those cutoffs. Equality is inside.

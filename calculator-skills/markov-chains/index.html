    Method and sources

    Rows are destinations, columns are starting states. Use Tⁿs₀ for a finite-step prediction. For the strictly positive two-state matrices in this lesson, the states tend to a unique steady distribution. Its entries sum to 1 and multiplying by T leaves it unchanged. Not every transition matrix converges. This simulator covers only the keys and matrix operations used in the lesson.

# #46, round 2: An editable math document that can check itself

**Decision:** Drop A (scan masking). Merge B+C: a printable, editable browser document backed by reviewed original building blocks, a deterministic mathematical checker, and explicit follow-through rubrics. Start with a narrow document/checker, not a general symbolic notebook or a Lean authoring workflow. The Maple-like experience is edit the maths, watch the dependent parts update, and see exactly what passed review. It is not general-purpose Maple parity.

## Smallest useful architecture

1. **Semantic document first.** Store stable, typed givens, math, figures, questions, accepted answers, workings and mark points. Give each block a durable ID. Model dependencies explicitly in the prompt (shared givens, required previous result/method, sanctioned alternate) and separately in the rubric (what student evidence each mark depends on). Render labels from the final order. Authoring doesn't require code, and the student paper never receives verifier source, key, hidden answers, or notebook outputs.
2. **One seeded resolver.** Reroll the whole connected component, not one dependent part in isolation. Store engine/toolchain and template revisions, seed, precision, angle units, variable domains and assumptions; compute a resolved snapshot and digest. Preview and freeze that exact snapshot into the paper and its separate key. Any edit, repin, reroll or rubric change invalidates the previous gate. Preserve MathLive/TeX notation separately from canonical mathematical representation.
3. **Typed, deterministic checks.** Use an allowlisted expression AST, per-item assumptions, explicit exact vs approximation and units, bounded size/processing and time/memory limits in a worker. Grade finite domains or exact algebra with small independent maths checks first. Treat counterexamples as disproof, not random samples as proof. When unsupported, ambiguous or in disagreement, show “Needs review”. Use pinned SymPy in CI when a second implementation adds value; require teacher review for diagram meaning, proof/method, explanatory prose and curricular judgement. Never run teacher/student JavaScript or Python merely to render a template.
4. **Follow-through is a rubric, not a checkbox.** Canonical answer from the authored givens; separately encode which prior student response may substitute, required domain and shown working, permissible alternate method, mark cap and reasons to withhold marks (including unexpectedly trivial or inadmissible input). Example on original g(x)=x²−6x+5: canonical gradient at x=2 is −2 and tangent y=−2x+1. A student who calculated −3 for the earlier slope but formed y=−3x+3 through (2,−3) may receive only explicitly authored method/follow-through credit, and only with the required evidence. The canonical key stays unchanged. If the earlier part is removed at authoring time, either keep it, omit the dependent part or offer a separately reviewed Given slope variant with a new rubric. Do not infer follow-through from words like “their” alone.

**What Lean adds:** A proof of a specific formal statement under explicit hypotheses, not of the author’s intent, natural language, worked reasoning, or the emitted PDF. A Lean model does not prove the separate JS runtime matches it unless that correspondence is addressed. If adopted later, pin Lean/mathlib, record exact statement and assumptions, check trusted axioms (no sorry or arbitrary added axioms), and follow [Lean's own validation guide](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/ValidatingProofs/). Don't block the MVP on formalizing the syllabus.

**Commercial guardrails:** Maintained notices/SBOM should cover exact shipped fonts, CSS, packages and separate content provenance. Author only owned/appropriately licensed original contexts, graphs, keys and rubrics; a publicly visible repo is not automatically a commercial-use grant. Confirm ownership and redistribution rights for each block. Do not take IB scans, prompts, worked keys, logos or screenshot assets into the author/AI pipeline; this is an original, curriculum-aligned product, not an official IB or Maple endorsement. GPL is not in itself a ban on commercial use; assess redistribution/integration obligations for the actual architecture. A pass is scoped to the declared checks and source revision, not proof of good assessment or examiner-equivalent marking. For example, the [GeoGebra product license](https://www.geogebra.org/license) requires a separate commercial agreement; use bank-owned diagrams and avoid assuming free classroom tools also grant product bundling rights.

**Prototype gate:** One original 3–4-part item, one safe deletion, one blocked dependency, one authored replacement, one wrong-but-consistent follow-through case and one invalid prior answer. Show changed total/criteria, the same seed reproducing the identical print/key, a genuinely new reroll and an explicit review state. Use A4 preview and separate key. Only then decide what needs a larger CAS, import standard or proof tooling.

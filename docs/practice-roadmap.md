# Practice: what to build next

Written November 2026, when practice first went live for students. Nothing here is a fault: every subtopic of all four courses has generated questions with worked solutions. This is the list of where practice is thinnest and which kinds of practice do not exist yet, kept so the work can be picked up if students find practice useful.

## 1. Thin subtopics

A student who practises one of these for long will start to see the same question shapes. "Ways of asking" counts the generators for the subtopic; a comfortable number is 8 or more, spread over at least two question types. The tables list every subtopic with 4 or fewer ways of asking, thinnest first. Subtopics shared between courses appear under each course that offers them, so fixing one often fixes several rows.

The full, current numbers are in [practice-coverage.md](practice-coverage.md), which the build regenerates. Rerun the figures from there before starting work, as this page is a snapshot.

## 2. Kinds of practice that do not exist yet

- **Prior-learning refreshers (level 0).** Only 1.5 and some of topic 2 (2.1, 2.2, 2.4, 2.6 to 2.9) have them. The rest of topic 1 and all of topics 3, 4 and 5 have none, so a student who is missing a prerequisite skill gets nothing easier than level 1.
- **Long Section B style questions (level 5).** No generator chains several subtopics into a 12 to 16 mark question. Today the "exam rung" serves real past-paper questions instead, which works but is a finite supply.
- **Sketching tasks.** The engine can draw graphs and ask a student to pick one, but cannot ask them to sketch. These would be self-marked against a drawn answer.
- **Proof.** Trigonometric identity proofs, and more than a few induction and contradiction proofs. These are self-marked, so they were kept few.
- **Specific skills noted as missing while writing content:** roots of unity (AA HL 1.14); Hamiltonian paths and cycles (AI HL 3.16); regression other than linear and quadratic (AI HL 4.13).

## 3. Smaller known limits

- **Worked solutions were read by eye at a few seeds only.** Every answer is checked by an independent verifier at 300 seeds, but the lines of working shown to the student are only checked for notation. An occasional awkward or unhelpful line of working is possible; report any that students find.
- **The lint rejects the word "null"**, so hypothesis-test questions say $H_0$ rather than "null hypothesis".
- **Practice worksheets print through the browser** rather than through Set up and export, so they have no cover sheet or Word version.

The recipe (generator, independent verifier, catalogue fragment, checks) is in [practice-engine.md](practice-engine.md), sections "Adding a generator and its verifier" and "Writing generators for AA HL, AI SL and AI HL content". Each batch is three new files and touches nothing else.

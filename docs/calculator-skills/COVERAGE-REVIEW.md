# Calculator Skills launch: IB paper scope and actual coverage

**Standalone library:** 59 inventory skills with separately scoped companions and beginner guides. Narration varies by lesson; see the [Paperclip handoff](../../calculator-skills/paperclip-audio/README.md) for the runtime contract and recording queue. These are TI-Nspire CX II-T non-CAS guided rehearsals on original examples, not full emulator firmware, full syllabus coverage, a collection of bank answers, or proof of support on any given physical handset.

**Where it belongs:** Calculator Skills is a top-level resource. In Practice, help belongs only with demonstrably eligible **original generated exercise** operations. It does not belong on actual or archived exam questions, even on calculator papers. Availability on a calculator paper does not make a symbolic proof, construction, explanation, or exact algebra step a calculator task.

## Scope corrections before making a broad public claim

- **CT46 t-tests:** has an independent, pooled two-sample branch using summary statistics. This is a legitimate AI SL 4.11 method; the additional one-sample/matched-difference branch is AI HL 4.18. Stock Watch demos one-sample, so signpost that honestly or defer an AI SL deep-link until it can open the correct branch. Use Pooled: Yes for the specified AI SL exam convention. [IB AI guide, printed pp. 57–58 and 61](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-applications-and-interpretation-guide-en.pdf#page=63); [TI two-sample t-test instructions, p. 3](https://education.ti.com/-/media/files/activities/us/math/statistics/t-test/t-test_teacher_nspire.pdf).
- **CT52 stationary points:** both SL courses can use the supported first-derivative-sign polynomial workflow. AI SL 5.6 expressly asks students to use technology to generate f′ and solve f′ = 0. AA SL 5.7–5.8 likewise includes numerical differentiation and sign classification. This does not teach a general second-derivative test or nonstationary inflection. [IB AA guide, printed pp. 63–64](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-guide-en.pdf#page=69); [IB AI guide, printed p. 66](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-applications-and-interpretation-guide-en.pdf#page=72).
- **CT53 and CT55:** AI SL 5.7 excludes kinematics in examinations, so CT53 maps to AA SL/HL and AI HL 5.13, not AI SL. CT55 is the AA two-curve area workflow (AA SL5.12) with optional AI HL transfer; AI SL5.5 directly names area under a positive curve and should use CT54 for compatible problems. Keep AA analytic setup/working requirements. [IB AA calculus, printed pp. 65–66](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-guide-en.pdf#page=71); [IB AI calculus, printed pp. 65–69](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-applications-and-interpretation-guide-en.pdf#page=71).
- **CT41 unknown normal parameter:** AA SL 4.12 explicitly asks for standardization and inverse work where mean or SD is unknown. AI SL 4.9 specifies both parameters given and no standardization for the usual inverse-normal requirement. AI's usual route is CT40, not CT41; CT41 remains browsable as enrichment. [IB AA guide, printed p. 59](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-guide-en.pdf#page=65); [IB AI guide, printed p. 56](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-applications-and-interpretation-guide-en.pdf#page=62).
- **Titles are not capabilities:** CT05 excludes recurring payments; CT27 compares routes to fill one least-distance entry; CT30 is boxplots/outliers; CT32 is quadratic regression only; CT34 is semi-log exponential only; CT57 is x-axis volume only. The [small catalogue-copy overlay](launch-copy-override.json) keeps CT IDs and URLs stable while correcting those descriptions and the cross-course claims above. It does not authorize any actual question placement or automatic mapping.

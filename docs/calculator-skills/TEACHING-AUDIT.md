# Calculator Skills: worked-problem audit

**Baseline:** first-assessment 2021 AA SL, AA HL, AI SL and AI HL; CX II-T non-CAS. This is an instructional audit and build order, not a claim of full syllabus coverage, a reproduction of exam questions or a physical device certification.

## What to retain from the older guide

Andy Kemp, *IB Mathematics Exam Preparation for Calculator Papers* (Texas Instruments, 2010) introduces the mathematics first; demonstrates why a calculator command is worth using; and finishes by weighing what the result means. Its original examples, old course labels, TI-Nspire 2.1 menus and 2010 exam assumptions are not content for this site. Do not copy its questions, figures or screenshots. Use the current [TI handheld guide](https://education.ti.com/en/guidebook/search/ti-nspire-cx), [IB AA guide](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-analysis-and-approaches-guide-en.pdf) and [IB AI guide](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/university-admission/pdfs/dp-mathematics-applications-and-interpretation-guide-en.pdf), and confirm exact keys on a school CX II-T or approved TI software when the path has changed. Ask the school which exam restrictions apply; the [IB calculator policy](https://www.ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/assessment-and-exams/exam-calculator-policy/) points schools to the current rules. Do not present simulator availability as exam permission.

## Lesson acceptance

- In the first screen and the first contextual voice beat, put a short **original problem** at the correctly tagged course level. State what is given, what to find, and why this calculator action is useful. Don't begin with an unmotivated menu or invent a story for a pure mathematical exercise. Number Entry, Handset Keys and Settings can use a short diagnostic case rather than a long question.
- Show the same case in the original prompt, handset readout, and final interpretation (including units, precision, bounds, axes or assumptions when applicable). Preserve exact values. Make a trip into the menu answer a decision the problem posed. A generic shortcut belongs in an optional note/chapter only if also demonstrated; no need to duplicate the lesson.
- **Try** uses the same kind of mathematical decision and calculator workflow with different original data. Match the command, required interpretation and course, not only the broad topic or the overall lesson title. An appropriate linked exercise can preload the setup in Try, but never the result. Watch remains independent of question data and should not solve any archived or assigned exam question.
- Audit each published route and each genuinely different scoped branch: correct Watch entry, relevant worked case, complete LCD actions, result/use, independent matched Try, source-backed syllabus and key path, matching narration. Use **ready**, **context-only refresh**, **realign Watch/Try**, **new workflow** or **needs device/recording verification**; shared player and signed voice are separate statuses. Don't claim any route passed the content review from its metadata alone.
- Existing signed speech remains valid only for its unchanged text. Add compatible visual context as an interim improvement where appropriate, and keep the contextual rewrite separate until approved Paperclip takes are checked. A visualization-only contextual pass is not a new narration pass.

## Prioritize existing mismatches before extra lessons

1. **CT46 (AI SL):** catalogue launches a pooled two-sample case in Try, but Watch opens with a one-sample/matched-difference case before the pooled example. Give AI SL a direct worked pooled case. Keep matched pairs within the appropriate AI HL path.
2. **CT54 (AI SL):** Try can be a wholly positive area task, but the default Watch starts from a crossing-axis signed-area example. Open a matching positive-area example for AI SL; retain signed-area interpretations where appropriate elsewhere.
3. **CT08:** original contextual pilot using unchanged signed audio. Watch locates a ramp's lowest point, catches the missed vertex and interprets its coordinates; Try uses a second ramp, checks the viewing window and then asks for the vertex. Visual context is new; recording remains the earlier procedural take.

Update [course scope](COVERAGE-REVIEW.md) and the versioned [syllabus matrix](full-matrix.md) only when a new or realigned lesson has teaching, firmware, math and browser evidence. Keep 2029 curriculum mapping separate.

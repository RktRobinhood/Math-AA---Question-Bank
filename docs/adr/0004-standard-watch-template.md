# 0004 · Build new explainers from one player template

Status: Accepted

Use one Watch player for new Calculator Skills lessons. Chapters, keys, spoken
lines, teaching holds and short explanations are lesson data; a calculator
adapter renders the genuine worked display and isolated Try on one shared handset. Screen view
crops the very same LCD from that handset; it is not a second playback. The player owns
Play/Pause, seek, captions, framing and fullscreen. Templates do not claim
unrecorded audio or slow a recording to fit. Keeping an independent player per
lesson made even small control bugs recur; a single full rewrite would risk
verified audio, calculator actions and Try. Adopt shared pieces in measured
legacy batches and keep their signed audio and exam boundaries.

See the [authoring template](../calculator-skills/WATCH-TEMPLATE.md) and
[contextual Practice boundary](0003-reuse-watch-for-contextual-practice-help.md).

# 0002 · Use a blank Scratchpad in eligible generated Practice

Status: Accepted

Generated Practice can offer a blank Scratchpad rather than passing the exercise into a solver. The student enters the mathematics; the calculator receives no prompt, seed, answer or grading state, and changing the question discards its work while reopening it on the same question retains the work. This gives students a usable calculator without changing the exercise or exposing a solution; Calculator Skills stays a separate place for worked examples.

Eligibility is a Practice decision, not an IB rule. For AA SL and AA HL, the launch is restricted to calculator-appropriate Paper 2–type generated work (not the AA Level 3 exam rung, explicit Paper 1 or Paper 3); this does not change the calculator policy on real AA HL Paper 3. AI SL and AI HL permit the Scratchpad in eligible generated Practice, including exact-answer tasks, which still say **Exact answer expected** where relevant. Real questions remain excluded under [ADR-0001](0001-keep-exam-archive-separate.md).
